Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The Hollow Hours Poster app is a personal tool operated by a single individual (the owner) to publish the owners own video content to the owners own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

When the owner authorizes the app, TikTok provides an access token and basic profile information (display name) for the owners own account. The app uses this only to upload and publish the owners own videos.

Data we store

The access token is stored locally on the owners own computer. We do not collect, sell, share or store personal data of any other person, and no data is transferred to third parties other than TikTok.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the apps access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services), after which the stored token becomes unusable and is deleted from the computer.
